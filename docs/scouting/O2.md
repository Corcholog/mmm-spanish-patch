# Read-ahead: O2 (4,713 entries + 3 executable strings, 24 files of ~200)

Scouted 2026-10-27 from the digest, a sample of every 3rd-5th entry of the whole script, full reads of the hazard spots, and quick scripts over `translation/O2/`. IDs are `O2:NNNN`. Proposals are not binding until approved into the style guide. The English patch is quoted only where it matters.

**What kind of file this is.** O2 is the **aftermath** of the contest and the festival: Ryo and Motomi have **broken up**, Kyosuke tries to patch things up, Misao finds out Reu is a Repliss, and at the end **Kyohei (Kyosuke's brother) turns up with a second Reu** (the "β type", later named Lease). Mostly new text: little reuse from O0/O1.
- **`branch_joins()` finds 0 joins** (verified). There are no shared-sentence jumps and no card-name lines. The branching is only 6 two-way menus (0176-0177, 1846-1847, 1941-1942, 2122-2123, 2650-2651, 4050-4051).
- **Only 296 entries (6%) are word for word the same as already translated O0/O1 lines**, and only 98 are longer than 8 characters. - **No Indian-poker card lines in O2** (no `\CG` codes, no 「…の手は」/「カードは」 lines): the only card game is the quick *Speed* (2524-2545), told in narration, no card names drawn by the game. Nothing to reuse from §5 of O1.
