Shared memory for the translator and reviewer agents: **what has happened so far and what recurs**, so each batch is written with the story in mind. Read it in full before every batch.

**Rules for keeping it useful**
- Facts only, from the text actually read; mark guesses as *(guess)*. Never invent plot.
- After each batch add **at most 6 lines** under "Scenes so far" (what happened, entry range) and update the other sections only when something new matters for translation (a name, a relationship, a running joke, a promise, a nickname, a speech habit).
- Keep the whole file under ~300 lines: when it grows, condense the oldest scene summaries into one line each.
- Terms and spellings are decided in `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md`, not here.

## Setting
- *My Merry May* (KID, PS2). Spring, during Golden Week (the holiday week at the start of May). A run-down two-storey wooden dormitory, the **Residencia Ami** (阿見寮), belongs to the Tsukumi Prefectural High School (県立津久見高校); about 30% of the students live in it because the modern concrete dorms ran out of rooms. A strict **encargada** (寮母) runs it; the narrator jokes she'd do something scary "at ESE moment" if she heard him badmouth her.
- A shopping street in town below the dorm, with the trendy casual-clothes shop **Monocromo** (モノクロ), celebrating its first anniversary with a prize draw (long queue at the till).

## Characters (what the text has shown)
- **Kyosuke** (恭介): first-person narrator ("オレ"), a student living in the dorm. Casual, a bit sarcastic, easily flustered; hides what he's buying for Reu out of embarrassment (blushes when teased).
- **Reu** (レゥ): a "Repliss" (レプリス), **two days old**, looks like a girl his age but acts like a small child; sleeps in Kyosuke's futon, no sense of caution, wears only the dress shirt he gave her. Soft, drawn-out childlike speech ("Vaaale", "¿quééé?"). Kyosuke is taking care of her in his room.
- **Hitoe** (榛名ひとえ, Hitoe Haruna): Kyosuke's **childhood friend** ("amiga de la infancia"); they bathed together as small children. Teasing, confident, gets him to talk; mock-formal with him ("señor Kyosuke") and calls herself "la gran Hitoe Haruna". First appears as an unknown voice (`???`) before Kyosuke recognizes her.
- **La encargada** (寮母さん): the dorm's matron, only mentioned so far.
